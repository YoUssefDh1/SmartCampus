## Conditions et candidature
To be eligible for the ISB **Bachelor's Degree in Business Intelligence** program, candidates must meet the following minimum requirements:
1. **Academic Qualification:** Hold a high school diploma (Baccalaureate).
2. **Subject Proficiency:** Good results in the **English** and **Mathematics** exams of the Baccalaureate, along with a strong level in both areas.
3. **Selection Process:** Admission is subject to review and approval by the Undergraduate Program Admissions Committee.

## Dates et frais
* **Registration Deadline:** September 15th
* **Program Start Date:** September
* **Online Registration:** www.isb.tn

## À vérifier
* **Minimum GPA:** Students must obtain a minimum GPA of **2.0 out of 4.0** per academic year to graduate.
* **Total Credit Requirement:** **180 credits** across **51 courses** over 3 years:
  * **Business Core Requirements (BCOR):** 33 courses totaling **131 credits** (Basic Activity Requirement Courses).
  * **Non-Business Core Requirements (NBCR):** 18 courses totaling **49 credits** (non-technical skills, leadership, and social sciences).
