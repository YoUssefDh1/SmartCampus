### Présentation détaillée
The National License in Management (Licence Nationale en Gestion) at ISB Sfax is a versatile three-year program taught in French that combines theoretical instruction, practical applications, case studies, workshops, and teamwork. It aims to develop:
* Critical analytical skills and problem-solving capabilities
* Open-mindedness and adaptability to organizational challenges
* Self-directed learning skills to stand out on the job market
* Progressive mastery of digital and emerging tools, including **Data Analysis**, **Power BI**, **Advanced Excel**, **Artificial Intelligence**, and professional management software (such as GPAO)

### Progressive Structure & Specializations
The curriculum starts with a common first year, followed by a progressive orientation in Year 2 and specialization in Year 3:
* **Year 1:** Common Core (Tronc Commun)
* **Year 2:** Choice between **Common Core** or **Accounting (Comptabilité)**
* **Year 3 Specializations:**
  * **Accounting (Comptabilité):** Master accounting standards and auditing tools
  * **Finance:** Financial management and market strategy
  * **Management:** Team leadership and effective decision-making
  * **Marketing:** Brand visibility and impactful marketing campaigns
  * **Human Resources Management (HRM / GRH):** Talent management and organizational dynamics
  * *(Also listed in study plan tracks: **Supply Chain Management**)*

### Mobilité internationale
Students can participate in academic exchanges or pursue double diplomas through ISB's international university network (including Université Laval, Concordia University, ICD Business School, College de Paris, IFAG, ESAM, Rennes School of Business, EPSI, Altınbaş University, Universiapolis, HEM, The American Business School, IDRAC Business School, Y Schools, CEFAM, FIGS Education, Westcliff University, Avila University, and Snow College):
* **Exchange Programs (ISB License Diploma — No tuition fees abroad):**
  * 2 years at ISB + 1 semester abroad + 1 semester at ISB
  * 2 years at ISB + 1 year abroad
* **Pursuit of Studies / Double Diplomas (With tuition fees abroad):**
  * 2 years at ISB + 1 year abroad
  * 2 years at ISB + 2 years abroad
  * 3 years at ISB + Master's degree abroad

### Débouchés et poursuite d'études
Graduates can enter the private or public sector, launch a startup/project, or pursue specialized professional or research Master's degrees in Marketing, Finance, Management, Accounting, or Human Resources Management.

### Target Roles by Specialization
* **Accounting:** Accounting Assistant, Junior Management Controller, Junior Auditor, Financial Assistant
* **Finance:** Junior Financial Analyst, Financial Analyst, Bank Customer Service Representative, Financial Advisor, Portfolio Manager, Finance Assistant
* **Management:** Assistant Manager, Project Manager, Administrative Manager, Team Coordinator, Development Officer, Executive Assistant
* **Marketing:** Marketing Assistant, Digital Marketing Manager, Communications Officer, Community Manager, Marketing Research Analyst, Sales Assistant, Brand/Product Assistant
* **Human Resources Management (HRM):** HR Assistant, Recruitment Officer, Training Officer, HR Manager, Administrative Personnel Manager, Talent Development Assistant
