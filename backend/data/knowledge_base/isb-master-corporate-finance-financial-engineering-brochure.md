### Présentation détaillée
The Master's program in Corporate Finance and Financial Engineering at ISB Sfax prepares students for finance careers and leadership positions. It combines academic rigor with practical application and integrates a **Digital Finance** specialization addressing digital transformations in the financial sector.

* **Practical & Applied Focus:** Builds skills to analyze financial situations, inform strategic decisions, and connect theory to field realities through senior industry practitioners, seminars, and certified training modules.
* **Corporate Finance & Financial Engineering:** Deepens mastery of business valuation, financial analysis, financial structuring, international risk management, and Islamic finance.
* **Digital Finance & FinTech:** Incorporates modern financial tools and digital finance topics, including Python modeling, big data, artificial intelligence, and blockchain.

### Mobilité internationale
Students can access international mobility tracks and Master's continuation options (subject to partner agreements and admission requirements):
* **Partner Network & Mobility:** IDRAC, YSCHOOLS, ICD, American Business School, CEFAM, EPSI, WIS, Montpellier Business School, Rennes School of Business, ESC Clermont, Collège de Paris, and Université d'Avila.
* **2nd-Year Master's Continuation in France:** Opportunities to pursue Year 2 in France at institutions such as ESC Clermont, Montpellier Business School, and Collège de Paris, including potential dual/double diploma options (such as a national degree alongside Montpellier Business School).

### Débouchés
Prepares graduates for roles across corporate finance, banking, market/investment finance, risk management, and FinTech:
* Financial Analyst / Credit Analyst (*Analyste financier*)
* Financial Engineer / Financial Engineering Advisor (*Conseiller en ingénierie financière*)
* Financial Risk Manager (*Gestionnaire de risque financier*)
* Portfolio Manager (*Gestionnaire de portefeuille*)
* Banker / Bank Inspector (*Banquier*)
* Chief Financial Officer / Financial Director (*Directeur financier*)
* Mergers & Acquisitions (M&A) Project Manager / Financial Engineering Project Manager
* Financial Auditor (*Auditeur financier*)
* Stockbroker / Market Intermediary (*Intermédiaire en bourse*)
* Head of Quantitative Analysis in Finance (*Responsable analyse quantitative en finance*)
* Head of Dealing / FX Room (*Responsable de salle de marché*)
* FinTech Startup Founder / Specialist (*Start up FinTech*)
