## Conditions et candidature
* **1st-Year Entry:** Open to holders of a fundamental or applied Bachelor's degree (*Licence fondamentale ou appliquée*), notably in management (*gestion*), economics (*économie*), private law (*droit privé*), or public law (*droit public*).
* **2nd-Year Entry:** Students who have passed the first year of an equivalent Master's program may apply for Year 2 entry, subject to application review.
* **Selection Process:** Based on pedagogical/academic criteria and a selection interview to evaluate the candidate's background and career project.
