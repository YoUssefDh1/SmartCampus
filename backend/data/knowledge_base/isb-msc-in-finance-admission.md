## Conditions et candidature
* **Academic Qualification:** Candidates must hold a Bachelor's or Master's degree in management, finance, or accounting, or an equivalent qualification.
* **Selection Process:**
  1. Application review by the Master's program committee.
  2. Selection interview with the candidate.
  3. Final admission decision based on both the application review and the selection interview.

## Dates et frais
* **Registration Deadline:** October 1st
* **Program Start Date:** October
* **Program Duration & Credits:** 2 years (4 semesters / terms, 120 credits), taught in English
