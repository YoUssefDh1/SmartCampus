### Présentation détaillée
The National License in Private Law (Licence Nationale en Droit Privé) at ISB Sfax allows students to pursue legal studies after the baccalaureate. Taught in French, it covers civil, criminal, labor, commercial, corporate, and business law while building legal reasoning, analysis, and drafting skills.

* **Understanding & Applying Legal Rules:** Helps students interpret legal texts, identify applicable rules for each situation, and construct structured legal reasoning.
* **Core Legal & Analytical Skills:**
  * Understand rules of civil, criminal, and labor law
  * Analyze legal cases and understand legal procedures
  * Draft legal documents using precise legal vocabulary and present clear arguments
  * Examine links between society and law, including how social changes shape legal rules
* **Comparative Law & International Context:** Includes an introduction to comparative law so students discover other legal systems and situate Tunisian law within an international context.

### Mobilité internationale
Students can pursue academic exchanges or double diplomas with partner institutions (including Université Laval, Concordia University, ICD Business School, College of New York, EUW, College de Paris, IFAG, ESAM, Rennes School of Business, EPSI, Altınbaş University, Universiapolis, HEM, The American Business School, IDRAC Business School, Y Schools, CEFAM, FIGS Education, STEC, ESG Clermont, Avila University, Westcliff University, and Snow College):
* **Exchange Programs (ISB License Diploma — No tuition fees):**
  * 2 years at ISB + 1 semester abroad
  * 2 years at ISB + 1 year abroad
* **Pursuit of Studies / Double Diplomas:**
  * 2 years at ISB + 1 year abroad
  * 3 years at ISB + Master's degree abroad

### Débouchés et poursuite d'études
Prepares graduates for legal careers across private and public sectors, as well as further studies (including Master's programs or competitive entry exams / concours):
* Business Lawyer (*Avocat d'affaires*), Magistrate (*Magistrat*), Bailiff (*Huissier*), Notary (*Notaire*) — via competitive exam (*sur concours*)
* Legal Assistant (*Assistant juridique*), Judicial Administrator (*Administrateur judiciaire*)
* Corporate, Bank, or Insurance Legal Counsel (*Juriste d'entreprise, de banque ou d'assurance*) — subject to access conditions
* Labor Inspector (*Inspecteur du travail*), Public Service Advisor (*Conseiller des services publics - ENA*)
* Foreign Affairs Advisor (*Conseiller des affaires étrangères*)
