### Présentation détaillée
The bilingual Bachelor (License) in Business Intelligence at ISB in Sfax combines data analytics, artificial intelligence, programming, and management to prepare students to turn data into actionable business decisions.

* **Data Analysis & Visualization:** Covers data analysis, data mining, data warehousing, and visualization to transform raw data into strategic insights.
* **Artificial Intelligence & Smart Solutions:** Includes foundational AI and generative AI training to design intelligent solutions for organizational challenges.
* **Bilingual & Hybrid Learning Experience:** Combines English and French instruction at the intersection of technology, programming, and business management.
* **Built-In Industry Certifications:**
  * **PCEP** – Python
  * **NVIDIA** – Generative AI
  * **PL-300** – Power BI

### Mobilité internationale

* **Internships & Corporate Network:** Professional partnerships allow students to complete internships, build networks, and gain real-world market experience.

### Débouchés et poursuite d'études
Graduates can work locally or internationally in private or public companies, agencies, consulting firms, or launch their own businesses.
