## Conditions et candidature
* **Academic Qualification:** Open to applicants holding a Bachelor's or Master's degree in management or engineering, or an equivalent qualification.
* **Language Proficiency:** Proficiency in both **French** and **English** is required.
* **Selection Process:** Admission is based on a selection interview, and each application is reviewed by a committee of academics and professionals.

## Pièces du dossier
* A detailed curriculum vitae (CV)
* Transcripts of grades
* Degrees obtained
* Documents outlining experiences, references, and internships
* Any other supporting documents useful for the examination of the application

## Dates et frais
* **Registration Deadline:** October 1st
* **Program Start Date:** October
* **Program Duration & Credits:** 2 years (120 credits), taught in French
