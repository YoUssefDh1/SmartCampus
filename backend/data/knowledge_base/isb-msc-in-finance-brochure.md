### Présentation détaillée
The Master of Science in Finance at ISB Sfax is a two-year English-taught program that combines theoretical coursework with practical experience supported by industry professionals, including a Digital Finance specialization.

* **Three Core Focus Areas:**
  * Corporate financial and tax management
  * Financial information and business valuation
  * Financial engineering operations
* **Financial Problem Analysis & Decision-Making:** Trains students to examine financial problems and root causes using structured analytical models, compare solutions, justify choices, and apply financial engineering decision-making tools in English.
* **Practical & Workplace Application:** Emphasizes defining action plans, organizing resources, leading teams, running simulation exercises, learning from sector practitioners, and completing an end-of-study internship or professional project.
* **Digital Finance Option:** Covers digital developments in the financial sector and their role in corporate finance, including FinTech, Big Data, Artificial Intelligence, and financial modeling tools.

### Mobilité internationale
Students can access international mobility and study-abroad opportunities through ISB's partner network, subject to partner agreements and admission conditions:
* **Partner Network:** IDRAC, YSCHOOLS, ICD, American Business School, CEFAM, EPSI, and WIS.
* **2nd-Year Master's Continuation in France:** Potential opportunity to continue the second year of the Master's program in France at ESC Clermont, Montpellier Business School, or Collège de Paris, subject to each institution's specific conditions.

### Débouchés
Prepares graduates for roles across corporate finance, banking, financial engineering, risk management, and FinTech, including:
* Financial Engineer / Financial Engineering Project Manager
* Financial Risk Manager
* Financial Analyst or Credit Analyst
* Portfolio Manager
* Bank Inspector
* Mergers and Acquisitions (M&A) Project Manager
* Financial Auditor
* FinTech Roles or Startup Creation in Financial Technologies
* With experience, progression toward roles such as Financial Director (CFO) or Financial Engineering Manager
