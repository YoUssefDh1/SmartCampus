## Présentation et informations importantes
* **Institution:** International School of Business (ISB), Sfax, Tunisia
* **Duration:** 2 years (4 semesters)
* **Total Credits:** 120 credits
* **Language of Instruction:** English
* **Prerequisite:** Bachelor's degree (or Master's degree in management or engineering, or equivalent qualification)
* **Start Date:** October
* **Registration Deadline:** October 1st

### Présentation détaillée
The ISB Master of Business Administration (MBA) is an interactive executive program designed to prepare future leaders and entrepreneurs for a global, technology-driven business environment.

* **Practical & Real-World Learning:** Focuses on practical applications and real business challenges aligned with current professional needs.
* **Artificial Intelligence & Smart Tools:** Integrates Artificial Intelligence across key business functions to support strategic decision-making, data analysis, automation, and digital innovation.
* **Human Leadership & Mentoring:** Keeps human leadership, communication, and decision-making at the core of the program, supported by personalized tutoring and individual project mentoring for participants from diverse academic and professional backgrounds.

### Key Highlights
* Solve real business challenges
* Use smart tools to support better decisions
* Receive personalized mentoring
* Develop leadership and management skills
* Connect with professionals from international industries

### Débouchés
Graduates can aim for senior management and executive roles in international and multinational companies, including:
* General Manager
* Administrative and Financial Director
* Senior Banking Executive
* Director in Charge of Information Systems
* Chief Management Controller
* Human Resources Director
