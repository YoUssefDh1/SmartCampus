### Présentation détaillée
The Licence en Sciences Économiques (parcours Business Economics) at ISB Sfax combines economic analysis, corporate strategy, and an international outlook. Taught in French, the program prepares students to understand how organizations adapt their strategies to shifting economic and market conditions.

* **Understanding Economic Transformations:** Covers globalization and international trade, innovation and its effects on companies, and ecological and digital transitions.
* **Connecting Economics to Corporate Strategy:** Explores competition, investments, organizational development, and business decision-making.
* **Developing Analytical Skills:** Helps students interpret economic data, link theory with real-world business issues, and evaluate how economic changes impact company choices.
* **International Perspective:** Connects local business realities with global dynamics and supports international certifications and academic pathways.

### Présentation détaillée
* **Understand the Economic World:** Decode major economic trends and their impact on businesses.
* **Think Like a Decision-Maker:** Master decision-making tools covering costs, investments, competition, and organization.
* **Master Data:** Use statistics, econometrics, and modeling to solve problems.

### Mobilité internationale
Students can pursue academic exchanges or double diplomas with partner institutions (including Université Laval, Concordia University, ICD Business School, College of New York, EUW, College de Paris, IFAG, ESAM, Rennes School of Business, EPSI, Altınbaş University, Universiapolis, HEM, The American Business School, IDRAC Business School, Y Schools, CEFAM, FIGS Education, STEC, ESG Clermont, Avila University, Westcliff University, and Snow College):
* **Exchange Programs (ISB License Diploma — No tuition fees):**
  * 2 years at ISB + 1 semester abroad
  * 2 years at ISB + 1 year abroad
* **Pursuit of Studies / Double Diplomas (With tuition fees):**
  * 2 years at ISB + 1 year abroad
  * 2 years at ISB + 2 years abroad
  * 3 years at ISB + Master's degree abroad

### Débouchés et poursuite d'études
Graduates can pursue research or professional Master's degrees in Tunisia or abroad, or enter business, consulting, public/private organizations, and entrepreneurship.
