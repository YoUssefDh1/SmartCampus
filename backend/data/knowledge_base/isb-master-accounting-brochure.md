### Présentation détaillée
The two-year Professional Master's Degree in Accounting at ISB Sfax deepens expertise in accounting, auditing, taxation, finance, and management control. Delivered by university academics and experienced accounting/tax professionals, the program prepares students for leadership roles in corporate finance departments or audit and consulting firms.

* **Core Disciplines:** Advanced and international accounting (IFRS), taxation and corporate law, corporate and market finance, management and management control, financial and organizational audit, plus economics, information systems, strategy, and Business English.
* **Preparation for the National Accounting Review / Expertise Exam:** Covers the full accounting review syllabus (CES / Examen National de Révision Comptable), supported by complementary leveling modules for Bachelor's graduates, intensive exam preparation sessions, potential scholarships for top Accounting Bachelor's laureates, and personalized tutoring from a permanent accounting faculty member.

### Débouchés
Prepares graduates for roles in companies, audit/tax firms, and independent consulting, as well as further examination pathways (subject to statutory/regulatory conditions):
* Chartered Accountant / Statutory Auditor (*Expert-Comptable / Commissaire aux comptes*) — after passing the national accounting expertise exam
* Internal Auditor (*Auditeur interne*)
* Management Controller (*Contrôleur de gestion*)
* Tax Advisor (*Conseiller fiscal*)
* Independent Consultant in accounting, auditing, taxation, finance, or information systems
