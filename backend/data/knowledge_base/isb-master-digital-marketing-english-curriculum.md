## Organisation et matières

* **Duration:** 2 years (Semesters 1 to 4)

* **Total Credits:** 120 credits

* **Language of Instruction:** English

## 1re année

### Semestre 1

* Introduction to Digital Marketing

* Branding: Brand Image

* Business English 1

* Graphic Design and Animation

* E-CRM

* Digital Media Planning (Advertising Creation)

* Direct Marketing and Email Marketing

* Analysis of Quantitative Tools in Marketing

* International Marketing

* Information Systems Design

* Algorithm and Data Structure

* Dashboard

* Internet and E-Commerce Law

* Digital Marketing Strategy

* Web Mastering Workshop I

* Analysis of Digital Consumer Behavior (Marketing Automation)

### Semestre 2

* Business English 2

* Website Referencing and Indexing

* Web Analytics and Traffic Management

* Mobile Marketing (UX / UI Design)

## 2e année

### Semestre 3

* Qualitative Marketing Techniques (Google Ads)

* Data Mining and Big Data

* Viral Marketing and Buzz Marketing

* Social Media Marketing

* E-Commerce and Electronic Payment

* Web Mastering II Workshop (E-Commerce)

* Project Management

* Business Games

* Business English 3

* Mini Project

### Semestre 4

* Internship Report and Defense
