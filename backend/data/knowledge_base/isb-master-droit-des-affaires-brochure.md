### Présentation détaillée
Offered through the International School of Law (ISL), the law section of ISB Sfax, this co-constructed professional Master's program brings together academics, lawyers, legal counsel, chartered accountants, magistrates, and business executives to prepare graduates for legal careers in companies, law firms, banks, and public bodies.

* **Practice-Oriented Legal Training:** Emphasizes case studies, role-playing/simulations, group projects, negotiation/arbitration/dispute management simulations, expert seminars, and professional internships.
* **Core Learning Objectives:**
  * Master core fields of business law
  * Support companies in strategic choices and advise leaders on commercial, financial, and tax operations
  * Prevent and manage legal risks
  * Conduct negotiations and handle economic disputes
  * Understand legal issues in Tunisia and internationally
* **Complementary Certifications:** Students can prepare for optional certifications (subject to issuing body conditions) in Compliance Management, Data Protection Officer (DPO), LegalTech & AI applied to law, Contract Management, Arbitration & Mediation, and Professional Legal English.

### Débouchés
Prepares graduates for roles depending on profile and experience (with access to competitive exams or regulated professions such as lawyer or magistrate subject to applicable rules):
* Corporate Legal Counsel (*Juriste d'entreprise*)
* Legal Advisor (*Conseiller juridique*)
* Business Law Consultant (*Consultant en droit des affaires*)
* Banking Lawyer or Tax Specialist (*Juriste bancaire ou fiscaliste*)
* Compliance Officer (*Chargé de conformité*)
* Contract Manager (*Gestionnaire de contrats*)
* Data Protection Officer (*Délégué à la protection des données — DPO*)
* With experience: Head of Legal, Regulatory Affairs, Governance, Risk Management, Mediation/Arbitration, or Legal Consulting
