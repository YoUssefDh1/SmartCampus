## Organisation et matières
* **Duration:** 3 years (6 semesters / terms)
* **Total Credits:** 180 credits
* **Total Courses:** 51 courses (33 Business Core Requirements = 131 credits; 18 Non-Business Core Requirements = 49 credits)
* **Included Certifications:**
  * **PCEP** – Python (prepared in Year 1)
  * **NVIDIA** – Generative AI (prepared in Year 2)
  * **PL-300** – Power BI (prepared in Year 3)
