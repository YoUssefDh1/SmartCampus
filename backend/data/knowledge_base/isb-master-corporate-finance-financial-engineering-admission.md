## Conditions et candidature
* **Academic Qualification:** Candidates must hold a Bachelor's or Master's degree in management, finance, accounting, or economics (or an equivalent qualification), with a solid academic background and strong academic record.
* **Selection Process:**
  1. Application review by the Master's program committee.
  2. Selection interview with the candidate.
  3. Final admission decision based on both the application evaluation and the interview.

## Dates et frais
* **Registration Deadline:** September 16th
* **Program Duration & Credits:** 2 years (4 semesters, 120 credits), taught in French
