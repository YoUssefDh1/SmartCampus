## Conditions et candidature
* **Academic Qualification:** Open to applicants holding a Bachelor's or Master's degree in management or engineering, or an equivalent qualification.
* **Language Proficiency:** Fluency in both **French** and **English** is required.
* **Selection Process:** Applications are reviewed by a committee of academics and professionals, and admission is based on a selection interview.

## Pièces du dossier
* A detailed curriculum vitae (CV)
* Transcripts of grades
* Degrees obtained
* Supporting documents for professional experience and internships, along with available references
* Any additional supporting documents useful for reviewing the application

## Dates et frais
* **Registration Deadline:** October 1st
* **Program Start Date:** October
* **Program Duration & Credits:** 2 years (4 semesters, 120 credits), taught in English
