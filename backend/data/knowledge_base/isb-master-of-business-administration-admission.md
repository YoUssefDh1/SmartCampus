## Conditions et candidature
* **Academic Qualification:** Open to holders of a Bachelor's or Master's degree in management or engineering, or an equivalent qualification.
* **Language Proficiency:** Fluency in both **French** and **English** is required.
* **Selection Process:** Based on a selection interview, with applications reviewed by a committee of academics and professionals.

## Pièces du dossier
* A detailed curriculum vitae (CV)
* Transcript of grades
* Degrees obtained
* Experiences, references, work placements, and any other supporting documents useful for the Admissions Committee

## Dates et frais
* **Registration Deadline:** October 1st
* **Program Start Date:** October
* **Program Duration & Credits:** 2 years (120 credits), taught in English
