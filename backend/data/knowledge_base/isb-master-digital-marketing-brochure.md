## Présentation et informations importantes
* **Institution:** International School of Business (ISB) — North American University Co., Sfax, Tunisia
* **Duration:** 2 years (4 semesters)
* **Total Credits:** 120 credits
* **Language of Instruction:** French (with Business English modules)
* **Prerequisite:** Bachelor's degree (or equivalent qualification)
* **Start Date:** October
* **Registration Deadline:** October 1st
* **Collaborations:** Google, Meta, Coursera, HubSpot

### Présentation détaillée
* **Industry-Expert Instructors:** Approximately 70% of instructors are active digital professionals bringing hands-on field experience into the classroom.
* **AI-Oriented Curriculum:** Artificial intelligence is integrated into more than 50% of the course content to prepare students for AI-driven marketing practices.
* **Flexible Pace ("Programme à votre rythme"):** Designed so both students and working professionals can study and work simultaneously.
* **International Mobility & Double Diplomas:** Opportunities for international mobility and pursuing the second year in France through partner agreements (including **Montpellier Business School - MBS** and **Y-Schools**, subject to agreements and eligibility conditions).

### Débouchés
Ranked among fast-growing global roles (World Economic Forum, 2025), graduates are prepared for positions such as:
* Relationship Marketing / CRM Manager
* E-commerce Project Manager
* Brand Manager
* Web Traffic Manager
* SEO & SEA Specialist
* Marketing Automation Specialist
* Growth Marketer (AI-Oriented)
* Social Media Manager
