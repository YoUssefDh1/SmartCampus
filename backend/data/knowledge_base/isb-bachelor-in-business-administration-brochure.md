## Présentation et informations importantes
* **Institution:** International School of Business (ISB), Sfax, Tunisia
* **Duration:** 3 years
* **Total Credits:** 180 credits (51 courses)
* **Language of Instruction:** English (100% taught in English)
* **Start Date:** September
* **Registration Deadline:** September 15th
* **Prerequisite:** High school diploma (Baccalaureate)

### Présentation détaillée
The Bachelor in Business Administration (BBA) offered by ISB in Sfax combines theoretical foundations with practical skills to prepare students for local and international management careers. The curriculum integrates digital technologies, online learning, and industry-tailored training across five core areas:
* Accounting and finance
* Marketing and market analysis
* Information systems management
* Business engineering and risk management
* Entrepreneurship and e-business

### Key Competencies Developed
* Creativity, communication, and project planning
* Financial management and market research
* Organizational analysis and strategic decision-making

### Mobilité internationale
* **Study Abroad:** Students have opportunities to study abroad through ISB's network of partner business administration faculties to gain international cultural and business experience.
* **Internships & Corporate Network:** Professional partnerships allow students to complete internships, build professional networks, and gain hands-on market experience.

### Débouchés et poursuite d'études
Graduates are prepared for roles in private or public companies, consulting firms, or independent entrepreneurship, both locally and internationally.

### Target Roles
* Business Leader (local or international level)
* Administrator
* Head of Operations
* Management Consultant
* Project Manager
* Independent Business Operator

### Academic Progression
Graduates can pursue Master's programs in Accounting, Finance, Marketing, or an MBA.
