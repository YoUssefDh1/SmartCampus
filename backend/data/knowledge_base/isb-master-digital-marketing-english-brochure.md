### Présentation détaillée
The Master's program in Digital Marketing taught in English at ISB Sfax blends hands-on digital industry expertise with an international outlook, equipping graduates and professionals with strategic and technical digital marketing capabilities.

* **Industry Practitioner Instruction:** Taught by experienced digital sector professionals who connect academic concepts directly to current business and market practices.
* **Practical & Technical Digital Coverage:** Emphasizes user experience and user interface (UX/UI) design, marketing automation, data mining, mobile marketing, web analytics, and digital media campaign planning.
* **Flexible Study Pace:** Supports balancing academic study with professional work or internships.
* **International Mobility & Double Diplomas:** Offers opportunities for international mobility and pursuing the second year in France through partner agreements—notably with **Montpellier Business School (MBS)**—as well as access to ISB's broader international partner network (including Université Laval, Concordia University, ICD Business School, College of New York, EUW, College de Paris, IFAG, ESAM, Rennes School of Business, EPSI, Altınbaş University, Universiapolis, HEM, The American Business School, IDRAC Business School, Y Schools, CEFAM, STEC, ESC Clermont, Westcliff University, Avila University, and WIS), subject to partner terms and admission criteria.

### Débouchés
Prepares graduates for roles in companies locally or internationally as well as freelance or consulting work, including:
* Head of Digital Strategy
* Digital Strategy Consultant
* Head of Relationship Marketing and Customer Relationship Management (CRM)
* E-Commerce or E-Business Project Manager
* Marketing Automation Specialist
* Web Traffic Manager
* Social Media Manager
* Paid Ads Specialist / SEO & SEA Specialist / Media Planner
