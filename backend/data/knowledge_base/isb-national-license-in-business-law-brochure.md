### Présentation détaillée
The National License in Business Law (Licence Nationale en Droit des Affaires) at ISB Sfax prepares students to understand the legal challenges facing businesses in Tunisia and internationally. Taught in French, the program covers commercial law, tax law, banking law, and corporate law while connecting legal rules to economic and management principles.

* **Understanding Corporate Legal Rules:** Helps students understand rules applicable to business activities, analyze legal situations, propose appropriate solutions, and follow changes in the law and their effects on businesses.
* **Core Business Law Skills:**
  * Understand commercial, tax, banking, and corporate law
  * Understand economic mechanisms and management principles
  * Draft legal documents and advise companies
  * Analyze and resolve legal problems
  * Communicate in a clear and structured manner
* **Practice-Oriented Teaching Methods:** Combines practical case studies to apply business law knowledge, thematic workshops with professionals, and internships that immerse students in real-world legal practice.
* **Opening onto International Law & Certifications:** Introduces foreign legal systems to highlight differences between legal practices, builds legal English vocabulary, and offers preparation for international certifications such as **TOLES**.

Students can pursue academic exchanges or double diplomas with partner institutions (including Université Laval, Concordia University, ICD Business School, College of New York, EUW, College de Paris, IFAG, ESAM, Rennes School of Business, EPSI, Altınbaş University, Universiapolis, HEM, The American Business School, IDRAC Business School, Y Schools, CEFAM, FIGS Education, STEC, ESG Clermont, Avila University, Westcliff University, and Snow College):
* **Exchange Programs (ISB License Diploma — No tuition fees abroad):**
  * 2 years at ISB + 1 semester abroad
  * 2 years at ISB + 1 year abroad
* **Pursuit of Studies / Double Diplomas:**
  * 3 years at ISB + Master's degree abroad *(also referenced with partner mobility tracks)*

### Débouchés et poursuite d'études
Provides a foundation for corporate legal careers, with specific roles potentially requiring further studies, additional training, or passing a competitive examination (*concours*):
* Business lawyer (*Avocat d'affaires*), bailiff (*Huissier*), notary (*Notaire*), magistrate (*Magistrat*) — depending on entry requirements and competitive exams
* Corporate lawyer (*Juriste d'entreprise*), tax specialist (*Fiscaliste*), legal advisor (*Conseiller juridique*), legal assistant (*Assistant juridique*), judicial administrator (*Administrateur judiciaire*)
* Associate in a law firm, expert firm, or consulting firm
* Legal framework / officer in a bank, insurance company, or public sector
* Labor inspector (*Inspecteur du travail*), public service advisor (*Conseiller des services publics - ENA*), foreign affairs advisor (*Conseiller des affaires étrangères*)
