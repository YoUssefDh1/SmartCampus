# Programme — Master of Science in Finance

Établissement : ISB (International School of Business - Sfax)
Formation : Master of Science in Finance
Année universitaire : Non indiquée dans la source
Source officielle : https://isb.tn/program-msf/
Vérifié le : 2026-11-28 par Youssef

## Organisation et matières
* **Duration:** 2 years (4 semesters / terms)
* **Total Credits:** 120 credits
* **Language of Instruction:** English
* **Specialization / Option:** Digital Finance

## 1re année

### Semestre 1 (Term 1)
* Contemporary Economy
* Business Law
* Taxation of Financial Instruments
* Business English 1
* Business Communication 1
* Investment And Financing Decision
* Financial Diagnosis and Cash Management
* Management Control
* Microstructure Market (Market Microstructure)
* International Trade Finance

### Semestre 2 (Term 2)
* International Finance
* Portfolio Management
* Stock Market Analysis
* Business English 2
* Business Communication 2
* Foreign Exchange Trading Room Simulation
* Financial Modeling: VBA Excel
* Applied Statistics in Finance
* Islamic Finance
* Insurance
* Company Evaluation

## 2e année

### Semestre 3 (Term 3)
* Financial Risk Management
* Derivatives Management
* Financial Engineering
* Banking Management
* Financial Modeling: Python for Finance
* FinTech: Big Data, Artificial Intelligence, Blockchain
* Leadership
* Research Methodology
* Financial Business Plan

### Semestre 4 (Term 4)
* End-of-study internship dissertation (internship or professional project)
