## Conditions et candidature
* **Academic Qualification:** Open to holders of a high school diploma (Baccalaureate).
* **Application Procedure:** Applicants should contact the ISB admissions office or apply online via www.isb.tn for specific required documents and enrollment steps.

## Dates et frais
* **Registration Deadline:** September 15th

## Organisation
* **Total Credits:** 180 credits over 3 years (6 semesters).
* **Track Selection:**
  * **Year 1:** Common Core for all students.
  * **Year 2:** Students choose either the **Common Core** or the **Accounting** track.
  * **Year 3:** Students specialize in **Accounting**, **Finance**, **Management**, **Human Resources Management (HRM)**, **Marketing**, or **Supply Chain Management**.
