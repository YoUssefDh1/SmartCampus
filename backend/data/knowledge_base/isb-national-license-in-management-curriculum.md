## Organisation et matières
* **Duration:** 3 years (6 semesters)
* **Total Credits:** 180 credits
* **Language of Instruction:** French
* **Structure:** Year 1 is a common core, Year 2 offers a common core or Accounting track, and Year 3 offers Accounting, Finance, Management, HRM, Marketing, or Supply Chain Management.
